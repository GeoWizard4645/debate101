Introducing Cascade

The flow follows your hands.

Cascade is Debate 101’s keyboard-first debate flowing app for Lincoln–Douglas, Policy, Public Forum and Parli. It runs in the browser and as a native desktop app, works completely offline, imports Verbatim cases and Excel templates, and reads and writes .ebb files — so a flow moves between Cascade and ebb without conversion.

Tab answers an argument · ⌘L links a response · ⌘⇧D jumps to a drop
Open Cascade in the browser Get the desktop app

Free · Open source · Nothing leaves your machine

Round Clock

Speech, prep and cross-ex clocks on official times, counting over when a speech runs long. Ending a speech walks the grid to the next column on its own.

Dropped-argument radar

Cascade watches every row across every column and tells you exactly what the other team never answered — with a one-key jump to each drop.

Voice Flow

Assisted capture listens for the words that start a new argument — “next off”, “turn”, “extend” — and lays them down as rows while you keep typing tags.

Block library

Type ;perm and press Tab. Your shells, frontlines and frameworks expand in place, shared across a whole team as one JSON file.

Round analytics

Coverage by side, time against the limit, density per speech, and a printable post-round report you can hand a partner or a coach.

Evidence tracker

Every card you mark becomes a cite entry with author, year and URL, exportable as a clean cite sheet for the post-round email.

Open anythingUpload .ebb, Verbatim cases (.docx), Excel flow templates (.xlsx), CSV, TSV, Markdown or plain text — on the web version too, not just on desktop.

Export anywhere.ebb (native and strict interop), CSV, Markdown, text, a cite sheet, and a landscape print layout for PDF.

Offline by designNo account, no server, no telemetry. Autosave keeps a copy in your browser so a dead laptop never costs you a round.
